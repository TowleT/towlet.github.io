Leave a Review

Thank you for reading. A short, honest review is the most helpful thing you can do for an independent book. It’s how the next reader finds it.

A sentence or two is plenty. Whatever you thought of the book, your honest view is what helps.

How it works

  1. Find your book below.
  2. Choose the Amazon store where you already have an account, or Goodreads. The store we think is yours is shown first.
  3. Sign in if asked. The review form opens for that book.
  4. Give it a star rating and write a line or two, then submit.

Before you start: who can review on Amazon

  • You need an active Amazon account in that country’s store. Amazon.ca, Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk and Amazon.com.au are separate stores, so use the one you normally shop on.
  • Amazon only accepts reviews from accounts that have spent a minimum amount in the past 12 months (about US $50 on Amazon.com, with similar rules in other countries). Purchases paid with gift cards or promotional credit may not count.
  • You don’t need to have bought the book from Amazon. If you bought it at a signing or a bookshop, you can still review it.
  • Amazon checks reviews before posting them, so yours may take a few days to appear.
  • If Amazon says you can’t review yet, Goodreads is a great alternative. It’s free to join and needs no purchase history.
